 Retention

      
Nothing Kept After the Job

      
The commercial promise is simple, and it is one of the strongest things about this model in a hospital security review.

    

    

      
No patient database
Nothing stored means nothing to breach. Most of what a hospital vendor assessment worries about does not apply.
Delivered as a draft
The letter arrives as preliminary. The clinician finalises it in DPC. That review is the clinical safety gate.
Purged after delivery
Audio, transcript and letter are deleted once filing is confirmed, enforced by a bucket lifecycle rule. Only the audit row remains.

    

    
The precise claim, and the one to make in a review: Arvi does not retain patient data beyond the processing window. Strong, true and defensible.

  







  

    

      
 Add-on

      
Patient Summary, on One Click

      
A separate, optional add-on. A clinician opens a patient in DPC, clicks once, and reads a short summary of the recent record. Seconds, not minutes, because someone is watching the screen.

    

    

      
One click in DPC
A Generate Patient Summary button in the patient profile. No new screen, no workflow to learn.
A short, sourced summary
A few lines of the recent record, with the documents it drew on and the dates, so any claim can be checked.
A view, not a record
Shown and discarded, regenerated on demand. It carries its own disclaimer and is never filed without review.

 Secure and Standard

      
Built on the Standards You Already Use

      
Nothing exotic. The integration runs on the same standards a hospital vendor assessment expects.

    

    

      
Standard FHIR
HL7 FHIR R4 (4.0.1), aligned with AU Core, at the two data boundaries.
Australian and encrypted
Audio in an Australian bucket, TLS in transit, encrypted at rest.
Modern auth
OAuth 2.0 SMART Backend Services, least-privilege scopes, no shared keys.
Accountable
Append-only audit of every call and delivery. Data hosted onshore.

    

  







  

    

      
 What We Need

      
What Each Side Provides

      
A short, concrete list. Most of the effort is confirming a handful of details, not building.

    

    

      

        
From InfoMedix

        
    
          
  • The patient as FHIR, and the consultation audio
  • A destination for the letter, a FHIR endpoint or a callback URL
  • The MRN, and its system identifier
  • Your LOINC codes for filing each letter type
  • A finalise action in DPC for the draft
  • For the summary add-on, your existing summary text
    • 
        

      

      

        
From Arvi

        
    
          
  • The published API, documented, with a sandbox
  • Transcription and letter generation, in the background
  • The letter delivered as a preliminary draft
  • No retention: everything purged after delivery
  • Standard, secure auth and an audit trail
  • Security evidence: ISO 27001, questionnaire
    • 
        

      

    

    
Both sides make calls to the other, so each issues the other a base URL and credentials.
