The Problem

Documentation Is the Tax on Every Clinical Minute

Across hospitals, clinics and theatres, clinicians lose hours to notes and letters instead of patients, and that content still reaches the chart late, inconsistent and incomplete.

Time poverty

Clinicians spend up to half their day documenting, much of it after hours.

Late, inconsistent records

Notes typed from memory hours later, quality and timeliness both suffer.

Costly downstream

Poor source documentation slows coding, referrals and discharge, the exact workflows hospitals depend on.

Our Vision

To become the autonomous operating system for healthcare: giving every clinician their time back, and letting every clinic run on its own intelligence.

 Add-on (either option)

      
Patient Summary, on One Click

      
An optional add-on that works with either integration. A clinician opens a patient in InfoMedix, clicks once, and reads a short summary of the recent record. Seconds, not minutes.

    

    

      
One click in InfoMedix
A Generate Patient Summary button in the patient profile. No new screen to learn.
A short, sourced summary
A few lines of the recent record, with the documents it drew on and the dates, so any claim can be checked.
A view, not a record
Shown and discarded, regenerated on demand. It carries its own disclaimer and is never filed without review.

 What We Need

      
What Each Side Provides

      
Whichever option you choose, most of the effort is confirming a handful of details.

    

    

      
From InfoMedix
  • The patient as FHIR (and the audio, for Option 2)
  • A destination for the letter, a FHIR endpoint or a callback URL
  • The MRN, and its system identifier
  • Your LOINC codes for filing each letter type
  • For the summary add-on, your existing summary text

      
From Arvi
  • The integration and documentation, with a sandbox
  • Transcription and letter generation
  • The letter delivered to the chart (draft, in Option 2)
  • Standard, secure auth and an audit trail

    

    
Both sides make calls to the other, so each issues the other a base URL and credentials.
