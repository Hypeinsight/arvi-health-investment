Application model

What the $30 plan earns, costed on real usage

A doctor pays $29.99 a month for a 10-hour block of recording and $39.99 for every extra block. Tell the calculator how many doctors are on the plan and how many buy one or two extra blocks. It then charges every hour of audio at Arvi's actual Azure cost, taken from the last three invoices, adds the services each doctor consumes and the fixed infrastructure floor, and reports whether the month makes money and how many doctors it takes to break even.

1 · REVENUE

Blocks sold

$29.99 per doctor for the base block, plus $39.99 per extra block. A doctor who tops up once pays $69.98 a month; twice, $109.97.

2 · HOURS

Blocks used in full

Base-only doctors record 10 hours a month, once-toppers 20, twice-toppers 30. Those hours are what Azure bills for.

3 · AZURE

Fixed plus per hour

The June, July and August invoices fit one line: $80.48 a month plus $1.86 per audio hour. More hours spread the fixed part thinner, so the cost per hour falls as the practice grows.

4 · OTHER COSTS

Services and floor

$15.40 a month per doctor for Redis, GCP and Pusher, stepping up at 100 and 500 doctors, plus a $2,000 a month floor shared across everyone on the plan.

Assumptions, and how to change them
Azure. The three invoices were June $396.00 for 169.89 hours, July $257.30 for 92.65 hours and August $185.26 for 57.69 hours. A straight line through them, $80.48 a month plus $1.8646 per hour, lands within $4 of each invoice. That line is what the calculator charges. It is the pay-as-you-go rate; a commitment tier would lower the per-hour figure further at volume. Services. $15.40 a month per doctor up to 100 doctors, $15.82 from 100, $16.86 from 500, covering Redis and the GCP and Pusher capacity that scale needs. Floor. $2,000 a month of fixed infrastructure, divided across every doctor on the plan.
