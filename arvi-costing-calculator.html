Token definition

What a Token Is

Arvi meters the Engine the way the industry meters speech: on audio duration. The token is the unit, and the price of a token carries everything Arvi spends to turn that audio into a clinical document.

Definition
Token. Arvi's unit of metered usage for the Engine service. One token equals one second of audio accepted into a transcription job, rounded up to the next whole second per job, with a minimum of 15 tokens per job. Tokens are counted only for jobs that complete successfully. The token price is fixed for the term of the agreement and is inclusive of the compute, storage and cache resources Arvi consumes to transcribe the audio and generate the clinical document; those resources are not metered or invoiced separately.
1 token = 1 s of audio60 tokens = 1 min3,600 tokens = 1 hr–
What the partner can rely on
  • Predictable and auditable. The bill is a function of audio seconds submitted, which the partner can reconcile against their own logs job by job.
  • One unit, no pass-through. Compute, storage and cache are Arvi's cost basis behind the price; they are never metered or invoiced to the partner separately.
  • Clear rules at the edges. Rounding to the second, a 15-token minimum per job, and no charge for jobs that fail.
  • Not an LLM token. A token here is a second of audio, not a fragment of text, and the rate card says so in its first line.
MEDICAL SPEECH APIs

Bill per audio minute

Amazon Transcribe Medical, Corti, AssemblyAI Medical Mode and Deepgram Nova-3 Medical all quote per minute or per hour of audio. Your token is the same base, expressed per second.

LLM APIs

Bill per text token

Anthropic, OpenAI and Google meter input and output text tokens. This is the meaning a developer assumes when they read "token", which is why the definition must lead with "one second of audio".

SCRIBE SaaS

Bill per seat per month

Heidi, Nabla, Lyrebird and Nuance sell a clinician seat. A partner comparing your engine price will convert it to a per-clinician monthly figure, which the engine model above does for you.

CLOUD

Bill per resource-second

GCP meters vCPU-seconds, GPU-seconds, GB-months and cache reads. This is the right basis for your cost model and the wrong basis for a partner's invoice.

Standard terms of a metered engine agreement

  • Unit and conversion table up front: 1 token = 1 audio second; per-minute and per-hour equivalents printed beside it.
  • Rounding and minimum per job, for example round up to the second with a 15-second minimum, so short retries are not free but not punitive.
  • Failed and rejected jobs are not billed; partial results on timeout are either billed for audio processed or not at all, stated explicitly.
  • Volume tiers or committed-use discounts, stepping the token price down with committed monthly volume, as in the rate card above.
  • Platform or integration fee per month, which is common for engine deals and covers support, environments and the FHIR endpoints regardless of volume.
  • Usage transparency: a per-job usage record (job id, audio seconds, tokens, timestamp) the partner can reconcile against their own logs.
  • Price review terms: the token price is fixed for the term; changes need notice, typically 60 to 90 days.
  • Scope of the unit: the token covers transcript and clinical document together; a transcript-only rate, if offered, is a separate line.
