What the $30 plan earns, doctor by doctor

Doctors do not use Arvi evenly. At Sydney Gut Clinic two doctors record around 30 hours a month, one around 8, and two under 5. So the calculator models three kinds of doctor, Low, Mid and Heavy, each with its own count and average AI and processing hours. Every doctor pays $29.99 for a block of roughly 10 hours and $39.99 for each extra block. Azure is charged at the rate fitted from Arvi's invoices, per-doctor services and the shared floor are added, and the page shows which kind of doctor makes money and which does not.

1 · DOCTORS

Three usage profiles

Real usage is skewed: Low doctors use a fraction of their block, Mid doctors use most of it, Heavy doctors go well past it. Set how many of each and their average hours.

2 · REVENUE

Blocks bought

$29.99 buys the first block. Hours beyond it are bought as extra blocks at $39.99, either whole blocks or, as a test, priced per hour. Hours paid for but not used are pure margin.

3 · AZURE

Charged on hours actually used

Invoices from June to August fit one line: $80.48 a month plus $1.86 per AI and processing hour. Unused hours cost nothing.

4 · OTHER COSTS

Services and floor

$15.40 a month per doctor for Redis, GCP and Pusher, stepping up at 100 and 500 doctors, plus a $2,000 a month floor shared across everyone on the plan.

Benchmark, Sydney Gut Clinic. On a shared 50-hour plan, the clinic used about 73 hours a month from March to August 2026, about 147% of its allowance, or roughly 23 hours over each month. That is about 15 hours per active doctor, but spread from under 1 hour to about 34. Monthly totals ran from about 50 hours (August) to about 90 (June).
Assumptions, and how to change them
Profiles. Low is under about 5 hours a month, Mid about 5 to 20, Heavy above 20. The averages used for each are inputs above; the Sydney Gut Clinic figures suggest 3, 12.5 and 30 as starting points. Azure. The three invoices were June $396.00 for 169.89 hours, July $257.30 for 92.65 hours and August $185.26 for 57.69 hours. A straight line through them, $80.48 a month plus $1.8646 per AI and processing hour, lands within $4 of each invoice. That is pay-as-you-go; a commitment tier would lower the per-hour figure at volume. Services. $15.40 a month per doctor up to 100 doctors, $15.82 from 100, $16.86 from 500. Floor. $2,000 a month of fixed infrastructure, divided across every doctor on the plan.
