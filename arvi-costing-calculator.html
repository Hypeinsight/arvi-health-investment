Assumptions behind the scenes
Azure AI. Three months of invoices fit a straight line: AUD 80.48 a month plus AUD 1.86 per audio hour (June 396.00 for 169.89 hrs, July 257.30 for 92.65 hrs, August 185.26 for 57.69 hrs; fit within AUD 4 of each). The per-hour rate looks like it falls with volume because the fixed part is spread thinner. "Safe rate" instead charges a flat AUD 3.33 per hour, above every month observed.
  • Every block is used in full: base-only doctors record 10 hrs, once-toppers 20, twice-toppers 30.
  • Add-ons per doctor: 15.40 up to 100 doctors, 15.82 from 100, 16.86 from 500 (Redis, plus GCP and Pusher capacity for that scale).
  • Shared floor of 2,000 a month, divided across all doctors on the plan.
